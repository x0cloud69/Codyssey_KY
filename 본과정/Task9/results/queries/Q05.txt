[Q05] INNER JOIN | 메뉴마다 카테고리 이름을 붙여서 확인한다 (category 1:N menu)
======================================================================

sqlite> SELECT c.name AS category, m.name AS menu, m.price
   ...> FROM menu m
   ...> INNER JOIN category c ON c.id = m.category_id
   ...> ORDER BY c.id, m.price;
