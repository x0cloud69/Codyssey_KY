[Q14] 서브쿼리 | 전체 메뉴 평균 가격보다 비싼 메뉴를 확인한다 (스칼라 서브쿼리)
======================================================================

sqlite> SELECT name, price,
   ...>        (SELECT ROUND(AVG(price)) FROM menu) AS avg_price
   ...> FROM menu
   ...> WHERE price > (SELECT AVG(price) FROM menu)
   ...> ORDER BY price DESC, id;
