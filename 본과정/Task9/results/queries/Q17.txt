[Q17] DELETE | 취소된 주문을 삭제하고, ON DELETE CASCADE로 주문 상세도 함께 지워지는지 확인한다
======================================================================

sqlite> SELECT (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM orders)                         AS orders_before,
   ...>        (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM order_item)                     AS items_before,
   ...>        (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM orders WHERE status = 'CANCELED') AS canceled;
+---------------+--------------+----------+
| orders_before | items_before | canceled |
+---------------+--------------+----------+
| 20            | 38           | 2        |
+---------------+--------------+----------+
(1 rows)

sqlite> DELETE FROM orders
   ...> WHERE status = 'CANCELED';
OK (2 rows affected)

sqlite> SELECT (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM orders)                         AS orders_after,
   ...>        (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM order_item)                     AS items_after,
   ...>        (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM orders WHERE status = 'CANCELED') AS canceled;
+--------------+-------------+----------+
| orders_after | items_after | canceled |
+--------------+-------------+----------+
| 18           | 36          | 0        |
+--------------+-------------+----------+
(1 rows)
