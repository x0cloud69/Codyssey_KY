[Q08] LEFT JOIN | 한 번도 주문된 적 없는 메뉴를 확인한다 (판매 이력이 없으면 oi 쪽이 NULL)
======================================================================

sqlite> SELECT m.id, m.name, m.price
   ...> FROM menu m
   ...> LEFT JOIN order_item oi ON oi.menu_id = m.id
   ...> WHERE oi.id IS NULL
   ...> ORDER BY m.id;
+----+--------+-------+
| id | name   | price |
+----+--------+-------+
| 20 | 밤라떼 | 6500  |
+----+--------+-------+
(1 rows)
