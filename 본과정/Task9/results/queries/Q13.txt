[Q13] 서브쿼리 | 주문 기록이 한 번도 없는 고객을 확인한다 (NOT IN)
======================================================================

sqlite> SELECT id, name, joined_at
   ...> FROM customer
   ...> WHERE id NOT IN (SELECT customer_id FROM orders)
   ...> ORDER BY id;
+----+--------+------------+
| id | name   | joined_at  |
+----+--------+------------+
| 11 | 한예준 | 2026-09-10 |
| 12 | 오하은 | 2026-10-01 |
+----+--------+------------+
(2 rows)
